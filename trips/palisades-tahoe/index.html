Parking and winter access

Palisades’ published 2026–27 program requires parking reservations on weekends and selected holidays. Recheck the actual date and base: a Palisades reservation and an Alpine reservation are not interchangeable. Resort lift connections, roads and shuttles can change with conditions.

For a new trip, first set dates, actual guests, airport/vehicle and lodging base. Then reprice the whole stay and confirm parking for the ski days.
