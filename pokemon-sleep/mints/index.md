How candidates are compared

A mint removes every nature effect, including useful bonuses. Candidates must improve their specialty output by at least 1%: gathered ingredients for ingredient specialists, triggers for skill specialists, or berries for berry specialists. Sort within each specialty by absolute daily gain; outputs from different specialties are not interchangeable. AAA filtering applies only to ingredient specialists. Existing neutral natures and already-minted helpers are excluded. Favorite status, current level, line and unlocked subskills remain visible so a mint can be weighed against existing investment; gain alone is not a perfect-build or team ranking.

Comparisons use the same 4,000-day simulation, 8.5-hour sleep, collections every 3 hours, fading energy, no camp, ribbons, events or additional Helping Bonus teammates. Carry includes evolutionary stage bonuses. Named ingredients are gathered output; random skill ingredients are excluded. Skill-trigger gains are not converted into team strength. Healing feedback, future leveling and the nature's EXP effect on future candy costs are outside this current-level comparison. In-game nature remains named the same; “neutral” describes its effects.

One mint per candidate, with no leveling candy or Dream Shard cost. Candidates are alternatives that share your saved mint inventory. Unknown forms, natures or unlocked ingredients are reported as unavailable. Small differences may reflect simulation sampling.
