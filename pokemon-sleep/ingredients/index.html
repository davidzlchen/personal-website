How output and resources are calculated

Saved-build maximum searches every level from the captured level to the account’s trainable cap, using the saved nature, subskills, and actual ingredient line. It is the highest modeled amount of the selected ingredient, not total mixed ingredients. A new slot can dilute another ingredient: its best level may be below the cap. Species, evolutions, seeds, and nature changes are kept fixed. Unevolved candidates show their current form’s production; evolution resources and effects are excluded.

Daily output uses the existing analyzer: 8.5 hours of sleep, collections every 3 hours, fading energy, no camp, no ribbons, and no additional Helping Bonus teammates. Only gathered named ingredients count. Random skill ingredients, healer feedback, cooking strength, and events are excluded. Estimates average 4,000 simulated days; small differences can be sampling noise. “Theoretical” describes this scenario, not a guaranteed daily harvest.

Candy and Dream Shards are incremental from saved EXP to the indicated target. Ordinary candy costs include EXP nature and overflow. Family candy and shards are shared: candidates are alternatives, and their budgets cannot all be spent independently. Handy Candy, type candy, clusters, sleep EXP, and Candy Boost are not used. Missing growth curves or slots remain unknown. Already-reached targets cost zero. Catching an unowned reference build has no known level, EXP, or resource cost.

The species benchmark uses Ingredient Finder M at 10, Ingredient Finder S at 25, Helping Speed M at 50, Helping Speed S at 70, and Helping Bonus at 80; only unlocked subskills apply. For mixed references, the selected ingredient is chosen wherever the species allows it; other slots use the smallest available quantity. Other legal natures, inventories, and subskill combinations can outperform this benchmark. Ingredient rate, speed, inventory, and slot quantities come from the existing production catalog and references. Neroli’s team guide discusses rotating ingredient farmers through a limited number of slots.
