How these comparisons work

“Max” means the strongest individual helper for the selected metric, first at its saved level and then with every supported helper at your trainable level cap. This is a level-only ceiling, not a perfect new Pokémon or a team prediction. Evolutions, seeds, nature changes, ribbons, and Candy Boost are excluded.

Daily output uses the same analyzer as Pokémon details: 8.5 hours of sleep, collection every 3 hours, fading energy, and no additional Helping Bonus teammates or camp. Main-skill recovery and teammate effects are not fed back into production. Ingredients have no cooking strength assigned. Helpers with unmodeled direct skill effects are excluded from strength ranking.

Locked saved ingredient slots are used when they unlock. Skill Level Up subskills increase the effective main skill level when newly unlocked. Production is an estimate; small differences can reflect simulation sampling. Cost estimates preserve captured EXP progress and next-level requirements, then follow the public growth tables. Handy Candy, type candy, clusters, and sleep EXP are not spent automatically.

Level and candy rules checked October 9, 2026. Sources: Candy EXP and Dream Shard tables, official level-cap and candy update, and production analyzer references.
